Why Choose FoodWise AI?

Food waste in institutions is rarely caused by indifference; it is caused by informational blind spots. Without structured real-time tracking, kitchen supervisors are forced to over-cater to guard against shortages.

Food waste can be reduced through better planning, accurate recordkeeping, transparent estimates, and informed decisions grounded in verifiable kitchen logs.

Redistribution Safety Protocol

  • 1. Sensory Check: Visual inspection, aroma evaluation, texture verification by head cook.
  • 2. Temperature Verification: Hot foods held ≥ 60°C or rapidly chilled ≤ 4°C within 2 hours.
  • 3. Food-Grade Transport: Clean, covered stainless steel pans with tamper-evident labels.
  • 4. Recipient Sign-Off: NGO/Hostel supervisor logs receiving timestamp and temperature upon delivery.

About the Project

FoodWise AI

An intelligent food waste reduction prototype designed to support smarter food preparation, surplus identification, and responsible redistribution.

1. Problem Being Addressed

Globally, approximately 1.3 billion tons of food is wasted annually. In large institutional environments—such as college canteens, university hostel messes, banquet halls, and corporate cafeterias—kitchen managers must predict daily diner attendance under volatile conditions (e.g., student holidays, weather changes, exam schedules).

Without transparent demand forecasting and prompt surplus alerting, substantial volumes of wholesome, freshly prepared food are needlessly discarded simply because surplus was detected too late to organize food redistribution.

2. Intended Users & Beneficiaries

College Canteens
Dynamic daily footfall management and batch preparation adjustment.
Hostel Dining Halls
Menu reuse protocols for dry grains and whole breads.
Event Caterers & Banquets
Immediate post-event redistribution routing to nearby shelters.
NGO Redistribution Partners
Verifiable food safety handover with temperature audit trails.

3. How the Application Works

The platform follows a closed-loop food stewardship workflow:

  1. Batch Logging: Food item, category, preparation time, and quantity are logged prior to service.
  2. Statistical Demand Baseline: If prior sessions exist for the item, the platform computes historical average demand; otherwise, explicit kitchen estimates are recorded.
  3. Surplus Identification: As service concludes, `Surplus = max(0, Prepared - Demand)` is calculated.
  4. Early Warning Alerts: If surplus exceeds configurable demonstration thresholds (>25%), immediate kitchen alerts prompt cold storage staging.
  5. Food Safety & Redistribution Sign-off: Supervisors perform sensory checks and log outcomes (Reused, Redistributed, or Composted).

4. Technology Stack

  • • Frontend Architecture: HTML5 Semantic Layout, Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ modular architecture).
  • • Design & Aesthetics: Specialty Coffee-inspired CSS Design System (Cream, Beige, Chocolate Brown, Sage Green).
  • • Data Persistence: Browser LocalStorage with zero external paid database dependencies.
  • • Data Visualizations: Chart.js 4.4.1 (custom palette theming).
  • • Verification Framework: Dedicated in-memory testing center with 16 automated assertions.
  • • Hosting & Server: Python 3.14 Built-in Multi-threaded HTTP Server.

5. Prototype Limitations & Future Roadmap

Current Prototype Status: Operates as a transparent statistical demonstration prototype. Thresholds and demand averages are based on sample means rather than multi-variate machine learning models.

Future Planned Improvements: Integration with college biometric attendance turnstiles, weather forecast APIs, automated IoT temperature sensor probes, and automated SMS dispatch to local food bank networks.

Food Safety Disclaimer: This software is a decision-support prototype. It does not replace certified public health food safety inspections or trained kitchen management judgment.
